# Governance

Tellscript is maintained by [Riverlabs GmbH](https://riverlabs.de) in Berlin. The maintainers decide on changes to
the format and publish every decision in the open.

## How decisions are made

- **Docs, examples and tools**: any maintainer may merge after one approving review.
- **The format** (`spec/`, `schema/`): a change needs an accepted **Spec change** issue, a pull request that updates
  spec, schema, checker, skill and docs together, and approval by two maintainers. It is listed in the changelog.
- **Breaking changes** before 1.0 are allowed but announced in Discussions at least two weeks before they are merged.

## Versions

The format follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Until 1.0, a minor version may break files written for
an earlier draft; the changelog says how to update them.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a record of careful reviews and accepted changes are invited by the existing maintainers. The goal
is maintainers from more than one organization before 1.0.
